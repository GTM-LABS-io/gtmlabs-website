Market Data & Why This Works

“Content repurposing” means taking a long-form piece of content and turning it into smaller, bite-sized pieces for social media. Search interest for content repurposing has grown rapidly, jumping from roughly 340 monthly searches in August 2024 to about 1,600 in August 2025.

Another data point: 61–67% of B2B companies run webinars, often for lead generation. Combine these trends and you get clear demand: companies running webinars need help repurposing that content. That’s the opportunity.

Step-by-Step Process Breakdown

Take a client’s webinar recording.

Transcribe the webinar.

Repurpose the transcript into multiple content types such as lead magnets, LinkedIn posts, email newsletters, and blog posts. Additional options include infographics and short-form video, though those can be more intensive to produce.

The strategy is to use existing webinars, turn them into transcriptions, produce multiple content pieces, and then feed those pieces into an email newsletter that promotes the next live webinar. This creates a flywheel: webinar → content → list growth → more attendees → more revenue. Many companies already have a large back catalog of webinars, so you can “resurrect” that library without asking them to create new material.

Required Tools & AI Automation

Use a transcription tool (e.g., Swell AI or similar). Upload a YouTube URL or a file to get a transcript.

Then use an LLM (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.) to generate the first draft of each asset:

Prompt to create a lead magnet: list key insights and maximize business value.

Move drafts into Google Docs for human editing before delivery.

Why clients pay:

They don’t know how to do this efficiently.

They don’t have time.

They want consistent output running in the background.

Offer unlimited webinar repurposing for a flat fee with revisions included. Repeat the same workflow for LinkedIn posts, newsletters, and blogs:

Extract 10 insights, then write one post per insight in mobile-friendly format.

You can mimic the client’s style by providing past posts as reference.

Automate the pipeline with tools like Make.com, Zapier, or n8n so a transcript can trigger the creation of multiple assets. Learning basic automation is straightforward with beginner tutorials.

Pricing & Revenue Potential

Because AI automations handle much of the back end, this can be a high-margin, one-person business. You can charge $1,000–$2,000 per month for a done-for-you package. Ten clients at $2,000/month is $20,000/month with no employees beyond your own time.

If you don’t want to learn automation in depth, you can hire an AI automation specialist (example given: Talent Fiber) to embed in your operation and build automations that keep outputs flowing while you stay lean.

Client Acquisition Strategy

Find companies running webinars:

On LinkedIn, search for webinar under Events and identify companies.

Open the company profile, filter employees by Marketing roles, and reach out via DM:

“Hey [Name], saw that you run webinars. I offer unlimited webinar content repurposing. Can I repurpose one webinar for free to show you my work?”

Use PhantomBuster for LinkedIn auto-messaging at scale: feed it a list of profile URLs and it sends your message automatically. Consistent outreach will produce responses.

Pro Tips for Maximum Value

Growing an email list is the most valuable outcome. Combine LinkedIn posts with lead magnets to grow the list on autopilot:

Post on LinkedIn with a CTA asking commenters to request the lead magnet.

Send them to a landing page to download the asset.

Track traffic and leads with a dashboard tool (example: graph.com) by connecting Google Analytics and a Google Sheet that captures form submissions.

Share a public dashboard link so clients can see traffic and leads over time.
